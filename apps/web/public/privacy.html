Orbit Privacy Policy

Last updated October 10, 2026

Overview

Orbit does not sell your personal information, does not show ads, and does not record your calls.

Orbit is a group video calling app built and operated by Myles Harris ("Orbit," "we," "us"). This policy covers the Orbit mobile apps for iOS and Android and the servers that support them (together, the "Service"). It explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

This policy takes effect on the date at the top. By creating an account or using the Service, you confirm that you are 18 or older and agree to this policy. If you do not agree, do not use the Service.

Orbit asks for camera and microphone access for calls, photo library access only when you choose a photo, and notification permission for call alerts. You can change these in your device settings at any time.

We get this information from you, from your device, and from other users, for example when a group member invites you by username.

We do not collect your real name, contacts, or location, or your email address unless you email us. We do not use facial recognition or collect biometric identifiers from your photo or video. Orbit contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

Video and audio calls

Orbit does not record, store, or transcribe your calls.

  • Calls run through Daily.co, our video provider. Your audio and video travel through Daily's servers in real time to reach the other people in the call.
  • Each call room is private, requires a token to join, holds up to 10 people, and is deleted when the call ends.
  • Recording and in-call chat are turned off.
  • Screen sharing is available. Anything you share is visible to everyone in the call.
  • Other participants could capture a call with their own device, for example with screen recording. Orbit cannot prevent this, so only share what you are comfortable sharing with your group.
  • To connect your call, Daily processes technical data such as your IP address, device type, and connection quality, and keeps session logs such as join and leave times. Daily handles this data under its own privacy policy.

How we use information

We use your information to run Orbit and nothing else.

  • Create your account and verify your phone number.
  • Schedule calls inside each group's call window and alert you when they start.
  • Show group members who is in a call and keep Live Activities and ongoing notifications current.
  • Record that you accepted this policy and confirmed your age, and respond to your support and privacy requests.
  • Prevent spam, abuse, and unauthorized access.
  • Diagnose bugs and keep the Service reliable.
  • Meet legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, sell it, or build profiles of you.

How information is shared

We share information only with the people you call, the providers that run Orbit, and authorities when the law requires it.

Other Orbit users. Members of your groups see your username, profile photo, group role, and whether you are in a call. Any signed-in Orbit user can find you by username and see your profile photo. Your phone number is never shown to other users.

Notifications and Live Activities

Call alerts can appear on your lock screen, so anyone holding your phone may see them.

  • Alerts and Live Activities show the group name, a countdown for scheduled calls, and how many people have joined.
  • On iOS, a Live Activity stays on your lock screen for the length of a call. On Android, an ongoing notification does the same.
  • You can mute a group in Orbit, change sound and vibration in Orbit's settings, or turn notifications off in your device settings.
  • When you sign out, we remove your device's push token from our servers.

Deleting your Orbit account

In the app. Open Account and tap Delete account. Deletion takes effect immediately, cannot be undone, and removes your account, profile photo, settings, device tokens, call activity, the photo of any group you own, and the invites you created or received. The app confirms when it is done.

Without the app. Email call.your.friends.on.orbit@gmail.com with your username and the phone number on the account. We will confirm the request by text message to that number, delete the account within 30 days, and tell you when it is done.

Copies in database backups expire on our hosting provider's backup schedule. We may keep limited information longer if the law requires it, or to resolve a dispute or abuse report. Our service providers keep their copies under their own retention policies.

Your choices and rights

You can see, change, export, or delete your information, wherever you live.

In the app. Change your username, time zone, notification settings, and profile photo. Leave any group, or delete your account. Group owners can remove members, edit group settings, and delete the group.

By request. Email us to:

  • Get a copy of the information we hold about you, in a portable format
  • Correct information you cannot change in the app
  • Delete your account and its data
  • Ask how your information is used, or restrict how we use it

We respond within 30 days and may first confirm your identity by text message to the phone number on your account. If we decline a request, we will explain why, and you can appeal by replying to our decision. We answer appeals within 45 days.

Do Not Track. Orbit does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not let third parties do so through Orbit. Orbit therefore does not respond differently to Do Not Track signals.

Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit, short-lived sign-in tokens, and private call rooms.

  • All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Call audio and video are encrypted in transit by Daily.
  • Sign-in uses one-time SMS codes and short-lived access tokens. Tokens are kept in your device's secure storage.
  • Call rooms require a token to join and are deleted when the call ends.
  • Access to production systems is limited to the person who operates Orbit.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you as the law requires.

Age requirement

Orbit is not for children or teens. You must be 18 or older to create an account or use the Service, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will delete it. If you believe a minor is using Orbit, contact us.
